Diagnostics

Loghaven includes diagnostic information under Settings > About > Diagnostics. This can help identify version and device-specific issues.

Do not share a Loghaven backup file unless it is needed for troubleshooting and you are comfortable sharing the information it contains.

Before reinstalling

Create a Loghaven backup before uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, resetting your device, or moving to another device. Android cloud backup is not used for the Loghaven database.
